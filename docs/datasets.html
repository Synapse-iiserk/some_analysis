Cross-Dataset Analysis & Correlations

Statistical relationships discovered by combining the 58 state-level datasets with petrochemical trade data.

GSDP vs Total Exports (r=0.670)

Strong positive correlation. The 10 states with highest GSDP account for 75% of tracked exports (agriculture + textile + SEZ + marine + meat). Larger economies have more diversified and competitive export sectors.

Unemployment vs GSDP Growth (r=-0.274)

Negative correlation. States with high unemployment (e.g. states with 20%+ UR) tend to have lower GSDP growth. Conversely, fast-growing states (Gujarat 19.6%, Kerala 17.6%) have moderate unemployment.

Per-Capita vs Growth (r=-0.076)

Weak negative. Richer states don't always grow faster. The catch-up effect is visible: low-per-capita states (UP, Bihar, MP) are now growing at 15-17%, while richer states grow at 13-15%.

Top 5 States = 52% of GSDP

Maharashtra (₹35.3L Cr), Tamil Nadu (₹23.5L), Karnataka (₹22.4L), Gujarat (₹19.6L), UP (₹19.2L) collectively contribute ₹13.6L Cr — over half of India's GSDP. This concentration is also seen in exports.

Gujarat: Highest Growth + Highest Per-Capita Tier

Gujarat recorded 19.6% GSDP growth in 2022-23 — highest among major states. Combines with 113 MMTPA refinery capacity, 17 SEZ locations, and the PCPIR (Petrochemical, Chemical & Petroleum Investment Region) zone.

Wide Income Gap (3-5x)

Per-capita GSDP ranges from ₹59K (Bihar) to ₹345K (Telangana/Sikkim). This 5.8x disparity drives migration, consumption patterns, and explains why some states are chemical/petrochemical markets while others are sources.

Methodology & Data Pipeline

Data Collection

31 datasets fetched via data.gov.in API using curl (Python requests times out on this API). 9 World Bank indicators via REST API. All data saved as JSON, processed to CSV.

Feature Engineering

Macro features aligned to monthly frequency. Lag features (1-12 months). Rolling statistics. Seasonal decomposition. Trade balance computed from export/import totals.

Model Training

Train/test split: 265 months training, 24 months test. 5 models: SARIMAX (auto-ARIMA), XGBoost (500 trees), LightGBM (500 trees), VAR (4 lags), ensemble average. All trained on 12 CPU cores.

Scenario Analysis

3 scenarios: Base (crude $84-88, INR 96-100), Bull (crude $65-70, INR 85-86), Bear (crude $95-110, INR 97-105). 12-month projections with monthly granularity.
