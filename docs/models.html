BEST PERFORMER

SARIMAX

Seasonal ARIMA with exogenous regressors. Captures quarterly budget cycles and monsoon-driven demand patterns.

Order: (2, 0, 2)
Seasonal: (1, 0, 1, 12)
Features: WTI, USD/INR, GDP, FDI, CPI

XGBoost

Extreme Gradient Boosting with lag features. Captures non-linear relationships between macro variables.

Estimators: 500 | Depth: 6

Max Lags: 4 (AIC selected)

Model Inference

Why SARIMAX Wins

Captures seasonal patterns (quarterly budget cycles, monsoon demand) while incorporating exogenous macro variables. The (2,0,2)(1,0,1,12) spec models both short-term autocorrelation and annual seasonality.

Ensemble Potential

Simple average of SARIMAX + XGBoost achieves MAE ~0.10, combining seasonal capture with non-linear feature interactions. Reduces single-model risk.
