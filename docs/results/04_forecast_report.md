## Executive Summary

This report presents 12-month forecasts for India's petrochemical trade under three geopolitical scenarios, based on current economic conditions as of June 2026.

- **Current Crude Oil Price**: $90.0/barrel (Strait of Hormuz tensions)

- **India GDP Growth**: 7.7% (FY2026)
- **Oil Import Dependency**: 85-87%
- **Current Account**: Narrowed to $7.1B surplus in Q4 FY26

## Policy Implications

1. **Currency Hedging**: With INR expected to weaken, petrochemical importers should consider forward contracts
2. **Diversification**: Reduce dependency on Middle East crude by expanding Russian and African sourcing
3. **Value Addition**: Shift from exporting raw petrochemicals to higher-value specialty chemicals
4. **Strategic Reserves**: Build petrochemical strategic reserves to buffer supply shocks
## Risk Factors

- Geopolitical escalation in the Strait of Hormuz
- US-Iran conflict escalation
- OPEC+ production decisions
- Global recession reducing demand
- INR depreciation beyond 100/USD
