Key Inferences for State-Level Strategy

Economic Concentration

The top 5 states (Maharashtra, UP, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Gujarat) account for 53% of India's GDP and ~55% of chemical imports. Any policy shock disproportionately affects these states.

Import Dependency

Gujarat has the highest petroleum consumption per capita (5.3 kg/cap) and major port infrastructure (Kandla, Mundra). Karnataka and Tamil Nadu have the highest per-capita petroleum use among non-refinery states.

Refinery Hubs

Refinery concentration in Maharashtra (Mumbai), Gujarat (Jamnagar/Koyali), and Tamil Nadu (Chennai) creates regional supply dependency. Eastern states like Odisha, West Bengal, and Assam have refining capacity but lower downstream conversion.

Growth Opportunity

States with <1% import share but high population (UP, Bihar) represent the largest untapped market. Investment in domestic petrochemical capacity in these states could capture 15-20% of national demand.
