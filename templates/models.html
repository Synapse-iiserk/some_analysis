Why SARIMAX Wins

Captures seasonal patterns (quarterly budget cycles, monsoon demand) while incorporating exogenous macro variables. The (2,0,2)(1,0,1,12) spec models both short-term autocorrelation and annual seasonality.

Ensemble Potential

Simple average of SARIMAX + XGBoost achieves MAE ~0.10, combining seasonal capture with non-linear feature interactions. Reduces single-model risk.
