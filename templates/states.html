Key Inferences from Real Data

1. Economic Concentration

The top 5 states (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Gujarat, UP) account for ~52% of India's GSDP (₹13.6L Cr of ₹26.2L Cr total). Any policy shock disproportionately affects these states.

2. Wide Income Gap

Maharashtra has highest per-capita GSDP (~₹2.5L), but states like Bihar, UP, Jharkhand have 3-5x lower. Per-capita inequality is a major drag on consumption growth.

3. Gujarat's Boom

Gujarat recorded 19.6% GSDP growth in 2022-23 — highest among major states. Combined with 113 MMTPA refinery capacity (highest in India) and PCPIR zone, it remains India's petrochemical capital.

4. Refinery Geography

Gujarat (113 MMTPA) + Maharashtra (26) + Tamil Nadu (22.5) + Andhra (17.5) = 75% of India's 257 MMTPA refining. Coastal Gujarat-Maharashtra belt is the petrochemical heartland.

5. Agriculture Dependency

MP, UP, Bihar, Rajasthan have high agricultural share of GSDP. As GSDP grows, manufacturing share typically rises — these states are at the inflection point.

6. Export Champions

Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh lead in agriculture and marine exports. Their port infrastructure (Kandla, JNPT, Chennai, Vizag) enables global trade.
