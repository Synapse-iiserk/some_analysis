Trade Analysis Verdicts

Where India is Strong

Organic chemicals (25.3% export share) and synthetic fibres (18.5%) are India's petrochemical strengths. India is a net exporter in specialty chemicals and catalysts.

Where India is Vulnerable

Polymers & Plastics (55% import dependency), Fertilizers (65%), and Petrochemical Catalysts (70%) represent critical gaps that expose India to supply chain disruptions.
